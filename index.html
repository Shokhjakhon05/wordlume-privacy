Wordlume — Privacy Policy

Effective date: 13 October 2026 · App: Wordlume: Word Connect Puzzle · package com.wordlume.game

Wordlume is a single-player, offline puzzle game. This policy explains what information the app handles and why. In short: there is no account and your gameplay data never leaves your device; the only data processing by third parties is what is needed to show ads.

1. Gameplay data

Your progress — your level, found words, coins, hints, daily-puzzle history, unlocked themes and settings — is stored only on your device, in the app's private storage. We do not operate a server that receives this data. It is removed when you uninstall the app or clear its data. If device backup is enabled in Android, the system may include the save file in your personal backup.

When you tap Share, the app hands a picture of your result and a short text to the Android share sheet; what happens next is up to the app you choose there. The app does not ask for your name, e-mail address, contacts, photos, files, precise location, camera or microphone.

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app shows ads served by Google AdMob: optional rewarded ads that you choose to watch (to receive extra hints) and occasional full-screen ads between puzzles. To serve and measure ads, Google and its partners may collect and process the Android Advertising ID, device and app information, IP address (which indicates approximate location) and ad interaction data. Ads may be personalised.

Where required by law (the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, Switzerland and similar regions) the app asks for your consent through Google's User Messaging Platform before requesting ads. You can change your choice at any time under Settings → Ad choices in the app (shown when applicable). You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in Android settings (Settings → Privacy → Ads).

Learn more: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

3. Analytics

The app contains no analytics SDK of its own and does not collect gameplay statistics.

4. Data sharing and sale

We do not sell personal data. We do not receive personal data from the app ourselves. The only third party that processes data is Google AdMob (advertising), as described above.

5. Children

Wordlume is intended for a general audience aged 13 and over and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

6. Data retention and deletion

Because gameplay data stays on your device, you can delete it at any time by clearing the app's data or uninstalling the app. There is no account to delete. For data held by Google in connection with ads, use the controls in your Google account and the Android ad settings.

7. Security

Data exchanged with Google's services is encrypted in transit. Local game data is kept in the app's private storage, which other apps cannot read.

8. Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new effective date.
